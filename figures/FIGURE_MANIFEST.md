# FIGURE_MANIFEST.md

Generated by `figures/make_figures.py` (run in the `jepa-omni` conda env, matplotlib 3.10.9).
IEEE-ish style: serif fonts, ≥5.6pt at final scale (all figures ≥8pt at their *intended*
print size — see per-figure width below; some in-figure annotation text is deliberately
smaller and is supplementary, not load-bearing), Okabe–Ito colourblind-safe palette,
hatch patterns duplicate every colour encoding so figures remain legible in greyscale.
Every figure ships as 300dpi PNG + vector PDF.

**Rule followed throughout**: every plotted number is either copy-typed from a real file in
this repository, or a trivial, explicitly-logged arithmetic derivation of two such numbers
(an average, an inverse for a rate, a subtraction to get a delta). Nothing is interpolated,
smoothed, or invented. Three places where the task brief's requested number could not be
found anywhere in `docs/EVIDENCE_LEDGER_V2.md` or `docs/METHODOLOGY_FORENSICS.md` are called
out explicitly below (Fig. 6 perception-prefix latency, Fig. 12 conversational-budget line,
Fig. 13 window count) — the real number found instead was used, and the substitution is
logged at the point where it happens, not silently.

Full per-figure source citations are also embedded as comments directly above each
figure's function in `figures/make_figures.py` — this manifest summarizes them.
