M4 JOINT-EXPOSURE FINE-TUNE — FIX FOR THE M3/M4b COMPOSITION FAILURE
Frozen: 2026-07-24
Source: checkpoints/m4_joint/last.pt (step 1500). best.pt (step 700,
  test_loss=1.130) is the checkpoint to use -- train_m4_joint.py tracks
  best-by-test-loss (same discipline established after the M4b overfitting
  bug).

── Why this run exists ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Gates (c)/(d) (checkpoints/m4b/PROVENANCE.txt) found that M3's connector
(trained on VGGSound, frozen) and M4b's projector (trained on EasyCom,
frozen) do NOT compose safely by naive concatenation in the shared LLM
input space -- (c) collapsed to F1=0.000, (d) degraded substantially.
Two cheap fixes were tried and failed first (dummy-token diagnostic,
stream-delimiter text) -- see checkpoints/m4b/PROVENANCE.txt for both.
This joint fine-tune was the fallback per the agreed decision tree.

── Data construction -- ARTIFICIALITY FLAGGED ──────────────────────────────
No real corpus pairs a VGGSound-style scene with EasyCom speech. Each
training step independently samples ONE VGGSound (clip, caption) pair and
ONE EasyCom (audio, transcript) segment -- unrelated, i.i.d., no temporal
or causal correspondence. 50% of steps target the VGGSound caption
(GRANULARITY_TAGS tag -- attend to M3 latents, ignore the present-but-
irrelevant speech block); 50% target the EasyCom transcript (new
SPEECH_TASK_TAG -- attend to speech, ignore the present-but-irrelevant M3
latents). This teaches STREAM ROUTING under a task tag, NOT reasoning over
two streams that are actually causally related in time -- a real
limitation carried into M4c planning, not a hidden one. See the 2026-07-24
scoping report on genuinely-paired EasyCom-only data (speech + a caption of
the SAME segment's actual AV scene) for the fix to this limitation.

── Architecture / training ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Trainable: M3Connector (39,404,032 params, re-init from
  m3_multigran_best/connector.pt) + UltravoxProjector (73,408,512 params,
  re-init from m4b/best.pt), BOTH unfrozen, trained together.
Frozen: M2 predictor, whisper-medium encoder, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct LLM.
  NO LoRA in this run (one variable, per instruction).
1,500 steps, per-example gradient steps (not batched -- variable sequence
shape per example), lr=3e-5 cosine, best checkpoint at step 700
(test_loss=1.130; final step-1500 test_loss=1.340, i.e. mild overfitting
past the trough, same pattern as M4b stage-1 -- best-checkpoint tracking
caught it automatically this time, no manual intervention needed).
