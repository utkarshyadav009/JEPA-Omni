IDENTICAL architecture/hyperparameters to the stage-1 (single-granularity)
connector frozen in checkpoints/m3_best/ -- only the training data and a

  - n_layers = 3, n_latents = 32, d_model = 1024, n_heads = 8, mlp_ratio = 4.0

Granularity conditioning: a plain-text tag is prepended after the 32
soft-prompt embeddings, tokenized with the frozen LLM's own embedding table

  gpt_action_brief      -> "Task: state the main physical action in one short sentence."
  gpt_action_detailed   -> "Task: describe the sequence of actions in detail."
  gpt_summary_brief     -> "Task: summarize the whole scene in one short sentence."
  gpt_summary_detailed  -> "Task: summarize the scene, setting, and context in a short paragraph."
  gpt_sound_acoustic    -> "Task: describe the sound itself and its likely source."

LLM: Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, FROZEN throughout (no LoRA).
M2 (frozen, feature source): checkpoints/m2_fusion_20k_best/step19000_peak.pt

── Failure trace: M3 clustered failures vs M2 (n=200) ─────────────────────
Hypothesis: clips M3 fails across ALL 5 granularities also perform poorly
on M2's own contrastive retrieval and linear-probe classification.

RETRIEVAL -- HYPOTHESIS FALSIFIED. Clean within-pool test (4000-clip gallery
  drawn from the same M3 common-test distribution; whole-pool sanity R@1=57.5%,
  consistent with the standard 1545-gallery baseline ~52%).
  Spearman rho(M3 caption-quality percentile, M2 retrieval percentile) ~ 0.10-0.15,
  borderline/non-significant. Worst-15 M3 clips have near-PERFECT M2 retrieval
  (mean percentile 99.9/100, rank 1-2 of 4000) -- statistically indistinguishable
  from the best-15 M3 clips (100.0). Retrieval measures distinctiveness, not
  correct understanding -- an initial naive test (extra queries inserted into
  the small fixed 1545-clip gallery) gave a spuriously inflated near-100% rank
  for nearly every clip and was discarded as confounded by gallery composition;
  see scripts/m3_retrieval_within_pool.py for the corrected methodology.

LINEAR PROBE -- HYPOTHESIS CONFIRMED. 309-way VGGSound probe reproduces the
  known ~54% aggregate top-1 (54.4% this run). Restricted to the 200 M3-eval
  clips:
    Spearman rho(M3 percentile, true-class rank)  = -0.36  (p=1.2e-7)
    Spearman rho(M3 percentile, true-class prob)  = +0.41  (p=2.3e-9)
  Worst-15-by-M3 clips: 20% probe accuracy, mean true-class rank 14.9/309.
  Best-15-by-M3 clips:  80% probe accuracy, mean true-class rank  1.9/309.

Characterization of the worst-15 (median train class count 507 vs corpus
median 575 -- only mildly long-tail, not the dominant driver):
  - ~1/3 audio-dominance mismatches: the VGGSound label names an action that
    is NOT the acoustically dominant signal in the clip (e.g. "shot football"
    clips dominated by crowd talk/laughter; "swimming" dominated by pool-
    equipment hum; "strike lighter" dominated by a person saying "okay").
    In these cases M3's caption is often an ACCURATE audio-first description
    that simply doesn't match the VGGSound label's framing -- not a model
    failure.
  - ~1/3 fine-grained acoustically-confusable long-tail classes (air horn vs.
    civil defense siren; sailing vs. rowing; elk bugling vs. fox barking;
    shofar vs. theremin; heartbeat/medical-beep vs. whistling).
  - ~1 likely VGGSound label noise case (label names one thing, both M3's
    caption and the M2 probe's top prediction independently describe
    something else entirely).
  - No evidence of low visual salience or off-distribution/animation content.

CONCLUSION: no vision-specific weakness signature. Failures correlate with
M2's classification/semantic-identification difficulty (not retrieval), and
are largely explained by genuinely hard/ambiguous content and label-vs-audio-
dominance mismatches. Per the user's decision rule, this supports proceeding
to M4 design over M2 modality-dropout.
